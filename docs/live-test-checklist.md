**Before any group:** Anki must be open with AnkiConnect running, and the console filtered to `jp-immersion` — several of these are only observable there. **Read the SERVICE WORKER console, not the page console** (`chrome://extensions` → the extension's "service worker" link): resolver logging runs in the background worker, so the page console shows none of it. Three live rounds were spent reading the wrong console before this was noticed on 2026-08-01. Items are ordered by value, not by where the bug was found. The checklist was first written 2026-08-01.

**B. Audio capture — run 2026-08-15; items 6 and 7 passed, the rest were rewritten from what it found.** (This heading previously said "items 4 and 5 passed"; corrected to match the item marks. Both passes came before that day's rework of the capture path.)
1. **Interrupted capture (SPA navigation).** Capture a word, then immediately navigate to the next episode before the chip appears. It must now have audio — the line up to the moment you navigated — and that audio must be from the episode you captured in, never the next one. (Until 2026-08-15 this correctly but unhelpfully produced an empty audio field every time.) Repeat with a seek instead of a navigation.
2. **Pause, then capture.** Pause mid-line for 30+ seconds, resume, and capture immediately. The clip must be the length of the line. The failure was a 44-second clip of silence with the audio at the very end; a console line reporting a clip trimmed to 20s now means something is still wrong and is worth reporting with what you'd just done.
3. **Skip around, then extend.** Skip forward and back a few times, capture, open the edit panel and drag both handles all the way out. Every part of the padding must be continuous with the line — no audio spliced in from wherever you jumped from. The padding is deliberately clamped at a jump, so a short pad here is correct behaviour, not a bug.
4. **Sentence end.** Capture several ordinary lines and listen to the last syllable on each card. The tail should be complete; the report was that the very end was consistently omitted. If it still clips, say roughly how much — the pad is one named constant.
5. **Waveform corruption.** Switch shows entirely (the case that swaps the `<video>` element), then capture and open the edit panel. The waveform should be clean, with no discontinuities, without a page refresh.
6. **Split-sentence audio, on a `[NanakoRaws]` file** — passed on 2026-08-15, kept as a regression guard. Passed live pre-rework; re-verify on current code. Capture from the first half and then from the second; both clips should cover the whole sentence, neither should run into the following line, and both should be near-instant.
7. **Ordinary single-cue capture** — passed 2026-08-15, kept as the regression guard on all of the above.

**C. The two-phase Anki send — new behaviour, not just a fix.**
1. Capture a word and watch the card in Anki's browser: it should appear **immediately** with every text field, and its `Audio` field should fill in a moment later. A card that never gains audio is a real failure; a card that gains it a few seconds late is the design.
2. **Undo during the gap.** Capture, then hit Undo before the audio lands. The note must stay deleted — no audio write should resurrect or recreate it.
3. **Capture two words in quick succession**, undoing neither. Both cards must end up with their own audio; the second capture must not strand the first.

**D. Edit panel.**
1. **Change word** — the fix for the reported corruption. Capture a card, open the panel, change the target word, save, and check the Japanese sentence in Anki is **character-for-character unchanged**, with only the bolding moved. Then reopen the panel and confirm it still reads correctly.
2. **Count the notes** while doing D1 — this is the unexplained half of that report. One capture plus one word change must leave exactly **one** note in Anki, not two.
3. **Typing.** Type a space and the letter "m" into both text boxes. The characters must appear; the video must not pause or mute.
4. **The rebuilt trim editor (2026-08-15) — entirely untested in a browser.** Click the waveform to move the playhead, press Space to play from it, press Space again to pause, and confirm the episode's own sound is muted while a preview plays and restored after. Then: drag a handle during playback; press I and O to set the edges at the playhead; turn Loop on and off; press Snap to speech and check it lands on the line rather than on room tone. Close the panel mid-playback — the audio must stop immediately.
5. **Open the panel IMMEDIATELY after capturing.** Two things to watch, both fixed on 2026-08-15 and neither verified: while the line is still playing the panel should say it is still capturing rather than showing anything, and the waveform that then appears must be THIS card's, not the previous card's. Leave the panel open and watch the right-hand padding grow to its full 3 seconds on its own.
6. **Editing after changing episode.** Capture a card, move to the next episode, then open the edit panel from the persistent control. The audio must still be editable — this said "no longer available to edit" until 2026-08-15.
7. **Extending the clip** — still never confirmed end to end. Drag the end handle outward and confirm you hear the extra audio on the *saved* card in Anki, not just in the preview.
8. **Anki closed mid-edit** — also never tested. Open the panel, make edits, quit Anki, hit Save: expect an error and your edits still sitting there. Reopen Anki, save again, confirm it lands.
9. **Chip timing** — a long merged sentence should now stay readable, and its English should be **the captured line's**, not the next one's. A single-word capture shouldn't feel like it lingers.

**E. The 2026-08-15 non-audio fixes — all six new, none seen in a browser.**
1. **First subtitle after a refresh.** Refresh the page mid-episode and click a word in the very first line that appears. It must be clickable; it used to render as plain text for its whole time on screen because kuromoji was still loading.
2. **Offset memory.** Adjust the offset on one episode, then go to the next episode of the same show and same release. It should start at the adjusted value. Then switch to a DIFFERENT release group in the switcher — that one should start from its own value (or 0), not inherit. An adjustment made before the file list loads must not be overwritten when it arrives.
3. **`{\anN}` tags and 。.** Watch a provider that showed either. No `{\an8}` anywhere on screen or in a captured `Sentence`; no 。 either, with a line holding two sentences reading as one space-separated line rather than two run together. `、`, `？` and `！` must all survive.
4. **Background English lines.** Find a moment with two English lines on screen at once and capture from the Japanese one. The `Translation` field must hold only the spoken line's translation. If the stray line is still there, note the show and timestamp — the rule keys off the caption file's own positioning, and whether Crunchyroll uses it on this content is the unverified half.
5. **Extension reload.** Reload the extension at `chrome://extensions` with a watch page open. Every panel should disappear and be replaced by one banner offering a Reload button; clicking it must bring everything back working, audio capture included.
6. **Nothing regressed.** Ordinary word lookup, capture and the English line, on a show that was working before.

**F. Correctness audit (2026-09-18, extended 2026-09-20) — F8 passed and F9 run on 2026-09-22; the rest not yet run.**
1. **Fast episode switching.** On any show, click to the next episode and immediately back, several times, before subtitles finish loading. The subtitles on screen must always belong to the episode playing (check the first line against the English), and the switcher's entry label must match. With the PAGE console open, `[jp-immersion] ignoring a TVEpisode block whose own URL names a different episode` is the URL check working, not a fault; the warning `carries no episode URL (no url/@id with a /watch/ id)` must never appear — if it does, Crunchyroll changed the block and staleness is back to the 5s wait.
2. **Remembered pick on an absolutely numbered season.** Shangri-La Frontier Season 2, episode 43: pick "Shangri-La Frontier Season 2" in the entry picker if asked. The file loaded must be `S2 - 18` / `S02E18`, never `(2024) - 26`. Reload the page: the remembered entry must load the same file.
3. **A pick with no matching file.** Pick an entry that has no file for the episode (any unrelated entry in the picker). The file dropdown must list the files and say "none loaded"; no subtitles appear until a file is chosen.
4. **Mission Recon.** Kaiju No. 8: Mission Recon must show the entry picker, not Season 2's subtitles.
5. **Display filters.** Bocchi the Rock! ep 6 (Netflix-derived file): no `（ひとり）` / `（虹夏）` labels on screen, including on the second line of two-speaker cues. Kimi no Na wa (NanakoRaws): no `((`/`))`.
6. **Film second half.** Any Bandai `.srt` film (Macross F movie 2, Zeta Gundam movies): subtitles must continue past the one-hour mark.
7. **Grunts.** Click `うっ`, `くっ`, `うわっ` in any episode: an interjection or "no dictionary entry", never 得る/来る. A stammered `ほ… ほかには` has an unclickable `ほ`.
8. **Audio-language switch (2026-09-20) — ✅ PASSED live, 2026-09-22** (KonoSuba 3, Japanese ↔ English; the console showed the stale block ignored and the old version's load discarded). On a show with a dub, switch the audio language mid-episode. Each audio version is its own `/watch/` id, so this is a navigation: subtitles must reload and match, or fail loudly — never keep the previous version's lines. If it reports "Couldn't detect the show/episode" and never recovers, that is premise (a) of the URL staleness check failing (the audit's Open Question 21, resolved 2026-09-22 — `project-plan.md` → Open questions → Resolved); run the collector below on that page.
9. **Measure the block itself (2026-09-20) — ✅ RUN twice, 2026-09-22;** results in `project-plan.md` → Architecture → Show & episode detection. Paste `scripts/collect-jsonld-blocks.js` into the console on a watch page, watch a few episodes (including a dub, a film and a special), then run `__jpJsonLd.report()` and `__jpJsonLd.save()`. It answers how fast the block names its own page after navigation, how many pages carry no block, and how many carry a block with no URL. Save the download into `fixtures/jsonld/` — it is live-captured data, the first JSON-LD this project has on disk.
10. **Every other way onto an episode (added 2026-09-22 — closes out show detection).** Reach an episode by autoplay-next, by clicking it in the episode list, with the back button, by typing its URL directly, and by moving to a different show. Each time, the subtitles must be that episode's, and a card captured on it must carry that episode's show/episode label. Then switch episodes rapidly and capture on each: the label on every card must match what was playing.
